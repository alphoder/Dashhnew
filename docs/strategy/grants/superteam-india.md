## Project: DASHH

**One-liner:** Brands escrow ad budgets on Solana and pay creators only
for views that Reclaim Protocol cryptographically proves are real.

**Live:** <https://dashhnew.vercel.app>
**GitHub:** <https://github.com/alphoder/Dashhnew>

## Why this matters for Solana India

1. **It's a real consumer use case for Solana** — not another DeFi
   primitive, not another L2 wrapper. Brands and creators with no
   crypto background can complete a campaign in 4 minutes.
2. **It's built on Indian infrastructure** — the founding team, the
   first design-partner brands, and the target user base are all
   India-based.
3. **It uses the full Solana stack** — Web3.js, Phantom Wallet, Solana
   Actions / Blinks, SIWS auth, and (in roadmap) Anchor escrow with a
   Squads multisig. Every piece of Solana's developer story is in
   production.
4. **Reclaim Protocol synergy** — Reclaim is a Solana ecosystem project
   and DASHH is one of its largest live integrations. Funding us
   strengthens both.

## What we've already shipped

- **45/45 Vitest tests passing** across payout math, settlement-window
  routing, terms builders, 3-strike ban logic, Reclaim adapter parsing,
  and disqualification rules.
- **22 production routes** under `/api/v2/*` with proper rate-limiting
  (per IP AND per wallet) and Zod validation at every boundary.
- **Sentry + PostHog + Turnstile CAPTCHA** all wired (P2 of our
  production roadmap).
- **Two-proof settlement model** — original work. Settles deterministically
  via cron. Brand auto-refund if zero creators verify.
- **Embed widget, case-study page, full docs site** — investor-ready
  showcase surfaces, all live.
