## 1. Equity split

**Each Co-Founder is allocated 20% (twenty per cent) of the total
equity of any future entity formed to carry DASHH forward** (currently
an Indian LLP, prospectively a Pvt Ltd if equity funding is taken).

The split applies to:
- Profit distribution from operations (once the LLP / Pvt Ltd is live)
- Proceeds from any future sale of equity
- Proceeds from any NFT mint (e.g. the DASHH Founder Pass)
- Grant money received in the name of the project
- Hackathon prize money received in the team's name

## 2. Vesting

Each Co-Founder's 20% is subject to a **4-year vesting schedule with a
1-year cliff**, starting from the Effective Date defined above.

- **Months 0–11:** 0% vested.
- **Month 12 (cliff):** 5% (one-quarter of 20%) vests in a single
  cliff event.
- **Months 13–48:** the remaining 15% vests evenly month-by-month
  (~0.4167% per month).
- **Month 48 onwards:** full 20% vested.

### Early departure

If a Co-Founder leaves DASHH before the 4-year mark — for any reason,
voluntary or involuntary — they **keep only the vested portion**. The
unvested remainder returns to the company pool and is redistributed
or re-allocated as the remaining Co-Founders see fit by majority vote.

### "Leaving" defined

A Co-Founder is considered to have left when any of the following is true:

- They formally resign their role, in writing.
- They cease meaningful contribution for **60 consecutive days** without
  prior agreement from at least 3 of the other 4 Co-Founders.
- They take full-time employment at a company in direct competition
  with DASHH (defined as Web3 influencer-marketing, zkTLS-verified ad
  platforms, on-chain creator economy).
- They are removed by a 3-of-5 vote of the OTHER Co-Founders for
  cause (fraud, breach of this agreement, criminal conviction, etc.).

### Bad-leaver clause

If a Co-Founder is removed for cause (the fourth condition above) OR
voluntarily leaves to join a direct competitor, they forfeit **all
equity including the vested portion**. This is the "bad leaver" rule
and it is mutually agreed upon.

## 3. IP ownership

All code, designs, content, branding, social-media accounts, domain
names, GitHub repositories, NFT collections, and other intellectual
property created in the name of DASHH or for the purpose of DASHH
**belong to the project as a whole**, not to any individual Co-Founder.

- The `alphoder/Dashhnew` repository is held in trust by the GitHub
  account holder, and all 5 Co-Founders are added as collaborators
  with admin rights.
- Personal forks are permitted for personal use only.
- Code written for hire (e.g. a Co-Founder's day job) is excluded.
- "Side projects" by a Co-Founder that are clearly unrelated to DASHH
  are excluded.

Upon LLP formation, all IP is formally assigned to the LLP via an IP
assignment deed (separate document).

## 4. Decision-making

Major decisions require approval by **at least 3 of the 5
Co-Founders** ("majority vote"). Major decisions include:

- Accepting any grant, investment, or equity sale.
- Spending project funds above **<<₹50,000 / $600>>** in a single
  transaction.
- Hiring or contracting anyone outside the 5 Co-Founders.
- Releasing new pricing (e.g. changing the 20% platform fee, modifying
  the NFT mint price).
- Adding or removing a Co-Founder.
- Selling, merging, or shutting down DASHH.
- Suing or settling any legal dispute on behalf of DASHH.
- Migrating funds out of the platform multisig wallet for any purpose
  other than scheduled creator settlements.

Day-to-day technical decisions (which library to use, what colour
the button should be, etc.) do NOT require a vote — the relevant
Co-Founder by role decides, and others can raise issues in the team
chat.

### Tie-breaker

In the event of a 2-2-1 split where one person is undecided, the lead
Co-Founder (**<<Vedant Singh>>** for the initial year) casts the
tie-breaking vote.

## 5. Confidentiality

Co-Founders agree to keep confidential:

- Business plans not yet publicly announced
- Discussions with potential investors, brand partners, or acquirers
- Internal financial state (revenue, runway, founder personal finances)
- Private keys, signing keys, env vars, or any platform credentials

Public information (code in the open-source repo, public Twitter
posts, public website content) is NOT covered by this clause.

This obligation survives departure from DASHH for **24 months**.

## 6. Non-compete

For **12 months** after departure from DASHH, the departing
Co-Founder agrees not to:

- Start a competing platform (Web3 influencer marketing, zkTLS ad
  verification, peer-to-peer creator-pay marketplace).
- Solicit DASHH's design-partner brands or registered creators.
- Hire away an active DASHH Co-Founder or employee.

Building products in adjacent spaces (general Solana dApps, general
Reclaim integrations, general influencer-marketing tools that don't
overlap functionally with DASHH) IS allowed.

## 7. Founder commitments — minimum

Each Co-Founder agrees to:

- Contribute at least **20 hours per week** during the academic year
  and at least **30 hours per week** during breaks, for the duration
  of the agreement.
- Attend the weekly team sync (currently Sunday 8 PM IST).
- Respond to team-channel messages within 24 hours during weekdays.
- Disclose any conflict of interest (e.g. taking a job at a related
  Web3 company) within 7 days of accepting.

Failure to meet these minimums for more than 30 consecutive days
triggers the "60-day inactive" clause from §2.

## 8. Compensation (current state — pre-revenue)

While DASHH is pre-revenue, no Co-Founder receives a salary.

When revenue (platform fees + NFT mint proceeds + grants) **exceeds
₹2,00,000 / month for 3 consecutive months**, Co-Founders are eligible
to vote (majority required) to begin distributing a fixed monthly
remuneration to active Co-Founders.

Until then, all incoming funds are reinvested in the project (RPC fees,
RPC + audit costs, marketing, design, infrastructure) OR held in the
multisig wallet as runway.

---

## 9. Disputes

Disputes between Co-Founders are first attempted to be resolved
internally via a majority vote of the disputants' peers (i.e.,
Co-Founders not party to the dispute).

If internal resolution fails, the parties agree to binding arbitration
under the **Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996**, conducted in
**Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India**. Arbitrator to be jointly agreed; if
no agreement within 30 days, appointed by the Madhya Pradesh High
Court.
