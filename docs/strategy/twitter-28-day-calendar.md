## What to NOT do

- ❌ **Don't buy followers.** Twitter purges them quarterly.
- ❌ **Don't follow-for-follow.** Same effect plus wrong audience.
- ❌ **Don't tweet "GM" 50 times a day.** Worked in 2022, punished now.
- ❌ **Don't shill in other founders' replies.** "Hey check out my project"
  in someone else's thread = block.
- ❌ **Don't promise utility you can't deliver** — "revenue share" / "token
  airdrop coming" are securities-law commitments.
- ❌ **Don't run multiple accounts.** Algorithm flags coordinated
  inauthentic behaviour.
- ❌ **Don't tweet your wallet address publicly.** People send scam tokens
  that hijack accounts.

## If you fall behind

The schedule is daily but if you miss a day, don't try to catch up.
Just resume from the current day's tweet. Twitter's algorithm rewards
consistency over volume — 1 great tweet/day for 28 days outperforms 7
in a row then silence.

**The thing that breaks Twitter growth is silence longer than 4 days.**
If you'll be unavailable, schedule via [Typefully](https://typefully.com)
or [Hypefury](https://hypefury.com) in advance.
