## Tax + compliance

See `docs/SECURITY.md` for operational security runbooks. For tax:

- **Get a CA who handles VDA tax** before receiving any money. Cost:
  ₹5k–₹15k/year. Don't skip.
- Every grant wire needs a **FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate)**
  from your bank.
- NFT mint revenue is **30% flat VDA tax + 1% TDS**. Plan for this.
- Once revenue > ₹20L/year, **GST registration is mandatory** (18% on
  platform-fee revenue).
